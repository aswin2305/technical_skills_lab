About Technical Skills Lab

Technical Skills Lab is a web-based coding assessment platform built to help college students develop and prove their practical programming skills across C, C++, Java, Python, and SQL.

Administrators create tests with reference solutions and test cases, assign them to student groups within a defined time window, and get instant, automated, objective grading — removing the delays and inconsistencies of manual evaluation.

Our mission is to make programming assessment faster, fairer, and more insightful for both institutions and students.
